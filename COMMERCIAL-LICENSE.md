# AIFP-4 / x405 Commercial License Framework

Copyright (c) 2026 AiFinPay.

This document describes the intended commercial licensing structure for AiFinPay-owned Reference Code, managed services, certification and proprietary integrations. It is not an offer and does not replace a signed agreement.

## 1. What does not require a commercial code license

A third party may independently implement the public x405 / AIFP-4 Specification, including for commercial use, under the Specification grant in LICENSE, provided that it complies with attribution, naming, trademark, patent and other applicable terms.

This is intentional: banks, fintechs, issuers, merchants, agent platforms and other companies should be able to implement x405 as an interoperable protocol without taking ownership of the standard or copying restricted Reference Code.

## 2. What requires a written commercial license

A written commercial license from AiFinPay is required where applicable for:

- commercial production use of substantial AiFinPay Reference Code licensed under PolyForm Noncommercial;
- hosted or managed services built from substantial AiFinPay Reference Code;
- resale, sublicensing, white-labeling or embedding substantial Reference Code into a third-party commercial product;
- proprietary enterprise modules or provider adapters supplied by AiFinPay;
- access to private or contracted provider integrations supplied by AiFinPay;
- managed routing, managed compliance integrations or managed production infrastructure supplied by AiFinPay;
- use of official logos, certification marks, provider badges or partner branding beyond nominative use;
- official x405/AIFP-4 certification or conformance programs where AiFinPay requires a certification agreement;
- service-level commitments, commercial support or other contracted production services.

## 3. No rebranding of AiFinPay-originated protocol material

A commercial license to Reference Code does not transfer ownership of x405/AIFP-4 and does not permit a licensee to present copied or substantially derived AiFinPay protocol materials as a newly originated proprietary protocol of the licensee.

Unless a written agreement expressly states otherwise, downstream products must retain the attribution required by LICENSE and NOTICE.md.

## 4. Expected production agreement sections

A production or enterprise agreement may cover:

- licensed legal entity and affiliates;
- territories and jurisdictions;
- permitted products, profiles and deployment model;
- transaction and customer scope;
- software, patent, trademark and certification permissions;
- provider-adapter and integration rights;
- fees, volume tiers, minimum commitments and revenue share where applicable;
- information-security requirements;
- audit and conformance testing;
- financial-regulatory responsibility matrix;
- data protection and subprocessors;
- support, service levels, incident response and business continuity;
- indemnities, liability limitations and insurance;
- termination, transition and migration;
- restrictions on credential sharing, circumvention, reverse engineering of proprietary modules and unauthorized network access.

## 5. Provider-neutral standard

The core x405 standard is provider-neutral. A commercial agreement may connect a particular Licensed Financial Provider to x405, but that provider does not become the owner of x405 or AIFP-4 by integrating with it.

## 6. Commercial contact and authority

Commercial rights beyond the public Specification grant should be requested through AiFinPay's official corporate contact channel.

Only a written agreement signed by an authorized AiFinPay representative grants additional commercial, managed-service, proprietary-code, network, certification or trademark rights.

## 7. Legal review

This framework should be reviewed by qualified intellectual-property and commercial counsel before AiFinPay signs production licenses in a specific jurisdiction.
