# 06 — Acceptable Use Policy

x405-enabled services MUST NOT knowingly be used to facilitate unlawful or prohibited activity.

## Prohibited or restricted categories

Subject to applicable law and provider policy, prohibited use includes:

- fraud, theft, scams or deceptive transactions;
- money laundering or terrorist/proliferation financing;
- sanctions evasion;
- stolen identities, cards, accounts or credentials;
- card testing, account farming or credential stuffing;
- unauthorized access to financial accounts;
- illegal goods or services;
- trafficking, exploitation or abuse;
- transactions prohibited by the applicable issuer, network, bank, PSP or jurisdiction;
- deliberate circumvention of merchant, country, MCC, velocity or compliance controls.

Additional regulated/restricted categories may be defined in each Provider Capability Manifest and Jurisdiction Pack.

## Enforcement

The Protocol Operator and/or Licensed Financial Provider may, where permitted or required:

- decline a route;
- freeze an Agent Financial Profile or credential;
- suspend a mandate;
- request additional information;
- preserve evidence;
- terminate access;
- make required regulatory or law-enforcement reports.

A suspension must not be represented as a determination of criminal liability unless made by a competent authority.
