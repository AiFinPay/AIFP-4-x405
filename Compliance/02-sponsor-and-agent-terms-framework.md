# 02 — Sponsor and Agent Terms Framework

This is a drafting framework for the agreement accepted by the Sponsor. It requires legal localization before execution.

## Required definitions

- **Sponsor:** verified natural person or organization that owns, operates or authorizes the Agent and controls or lawfully accesses the funding source.
- **Agent:** registered autonomous software identity bound through AIFP-3/x405.
- **Delegated Authority:** machine-readable mandate granted by Sponsor.
- **Payment Credential:** tokenized or provider-scoped payment capability linked to an Agent Financial Profile.
- **Licensed Financial Provider:** eligible issuer, bank, PSP, EMI/PI, processor, VASP or other regulated execution provider.

## Sponsor acknowledgements

The Sponsor should expressly acknowledge that it:

1. has authority to bind the funding source and create the Agent mandate;
2. voluntarily authorizes the Agent to initiate payment actions within configured limits;
3. is responsible for the Agent configuration, connected tools, prompts, software and access controls under Sponsor control;
4. must set appropriate transaction, merchant, MCC, country, currency, rail and velocity limits;
5. must promptly suspend/revoke an Agent or credential after suspected compromise;
6. must use x405 only for lawful purposes and in supported jurisdictions;
7. understands that regulated providers may decline, hold, reverse, investigate or report transactions as required by law or network rules.

## x405 service boundary

Terms should state that AiFinPay/x405 provides protocol and technology functions and does not, solely by providing those functions, act as the card issuer, deposit-taking bank, custodian or money transmitter.

Any regulated service is supplied by the applicable Licensed Financial Provider under the relevant terms and law.

## Mandatory-law savings clause

Nothing in the agreement excludes or limits rights or liabilities that cannot legally be excluded. Provider/network rules and mandatory payment law prevail where applicable.
