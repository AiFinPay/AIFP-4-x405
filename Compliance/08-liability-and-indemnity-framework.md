# 08 — Liability and Indemnity Framework

This document provides drafting principles only. Local counsel must determine enforceability.

## Sponsor responsibility area

To the extent permitted by law, Sponsor terms may allocate responsibility to the Sponsor for:

- Agent configuration and software under Sponsor control;
- prompts, tools, plugins, MCP servers and integrations chosen by Sponsor;
- mandate limits and permissions;
- lawful purpose of transactions;
- beneficiary/merchant data supplied by Sponsor or Agent;
- timely revocation after known compromise;
- tax/accounting obligations of Sponsor;
- use outside approved jurisdictions or provider terms.

## Protocol Operator limitation area

Terms may limit responsibility, where legally permitted, for:

- merchant performance or product quality;
- third-party model/tool/plugin behavior;
- issuer, bank, network, blockchain or PSP outages;
- provider declines/holds required by compliance;
- third-party FX or network pricing;
- properly executed transactions that matched an active Sponsor mandate.

## Required carve-outs

A limitation clause MUST preserve liability/rights that cannot be excluded under applicable law. Counsel should specifically assess fraud, wilful misconduct, gross negligence, data/security obligations, statutory payment-user rights and regulatory duties.

## Indemnity concept

For B2B use, the Sponsor may indemnify the Protocol Operator for third-party claims caused by Sponsor's unlawful use, breach of AUP, unauthorized delegation, infringement, tax obligations or material breach, subject to negotiated exclusions, control of defense and applicable law.

No indemnity should be described as eliminating the Protocol Operator's own mandatory legal responsibilities.
